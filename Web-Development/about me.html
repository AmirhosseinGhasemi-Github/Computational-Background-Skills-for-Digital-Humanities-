HANI NASROLLAHI

About Hani Nasrollahi

Hani Nasrollahi, born on February 4, 1990, in Tehran, is a graduate of Graphic Design and a talented painter, performer, and graphic artist. She entered the world of painting in 2011 and held her first group exhibition at Saba Cultural Center in the same year. In 2016, as a member of the Alborz Painters Association, she participated in her second group exhibition. Since 2017, she has expanded her artistic endeavors to include performance art and theater acting.

Her notable works include Oreshima, The Fisherman in the Pigeon House, The Birth of Light in the Mirror of the Divan, Japanese Theater Performance, Sense of Art in Dubai, Dreamquake, Then That Look at Roberoo Mansion, and the short film The Black Bubble.
